About

I’m Tristan, a Georgia Tech computer scientist working at the intersection of machine learning, control, and autonomous systems engineering. I build deployable, high-performance systems that operate under real-world physical and computational constraints.

My background includes defense-sponsored work in airborne perception and real-time autonomy. I’ve built learning and optimization pipelines (evolutionary search + surrogate modeling), deployed perception systems on large-scale aerial data, and engineered decision pipelines where latency, robustness, and integration across subsystems matter.

I’m especially focused on learning-based decision systems: deep reinforcement learning, multi-agent coordination, and the design of collaborative/adversarial robotic agents operating under uncertainty. Long term, I want to engineer scalable autonomous platforms that unify perception, learning, and control into cohesive, self-improving systems.
